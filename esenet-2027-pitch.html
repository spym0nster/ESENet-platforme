The Vision

"Why should this connection exist only for one day?"

ESENet has always been about networking, connecting people, and creating value. Today it's experienced mainly through the annual Talent Fair — students meet companies, startups, alumni and professionals, and build connections, for one day.

ESENet 2027 takes the concept beyond the physical event: a digital platform connecting ESEN students, alumni, companies and opportunities all year round.

The goal is simple: turn ESENet from an annual Talent Fair into a continuous talent and networking ecosystem.
The Problem

Two sides looking for each other, with no bridge between them.

Students are looking for

  • Internships
  • Summer internships
  • Alternance opportunities
  • First jobs
  • Freelance work & projects
  • Competitions & challenges
  • Networking opportunities

Companies are looking for

  • Interns & PFE students
  • Junior talent
  • Developers
  • Data / BI profiles
  • Marketing profiles
  • Business profiles
  • Future employees
The problem isn't the lack of opportunities or talent. It's the connection between the two. Today students find offers through LinkedIn, Facebook groups, personal contacts or random messages — there's no dedicated space for the ESEN ecosystem.
The Solution

ESENet Platform

Companies and startups publish opportunities directly on ESENet. Students build a professional profile, discover relevant opportunities, apply, and connect with recruiters and alumni.

Instead of happening once a year, ESENet becomes something students and companies use every day.

The Bigger Idea

Every connection creates another opportunity. A student finds an internship. That student becomes an employee, then an alumnus. That alumnus returns as a recruiter, mentor or partner — and recruits another student. That's the network effect.
